Known limitations

Please check these before relying on MyPDF for important documents.

File size depends on your device

Large files are processed in your device's memory. On phones, keep files under about 100 MB or 300 pages. Bigger files may be slow or may crash the browser tab.

No password-protected PDFs

Encrypted or password-protected PDFs aren't supported. Remove the password first, using an app that has it.

Signatures are visual, not certified

Sign PDF places an image of your signature. It is not a certified digital signature (PKI / eIDAS), and some legal or government documents require one.

Bookmarks and forms may be lost

Merge, Split and Organize keep the page content, but bookmarks (the outline) are dropped and fillable form fields may stop working.

Image format support varies by browser

iPhone HEIC photos only work in Safari. In other browsers, convert them to JPG first. Animated GIFs use only the first frame.

No OCR or text editing

MyPDF can't recognise text in scans or edit the existing text inside a PDF. Compress works by turning pages into pictures, so text in a compressed PDF can't be selected.

Office conversions are simplified

Word, Excel and PowerPoint files are converted in your browser, not by Microsoft Office. Text, tables and pictures come through, but complex layouts, fonts, charts and effects may look different. Always check the result.

Rendering differences

PDF to JPG uses a browser renderer. PDFs with unusual fonts or complex graphics may look slightly different from Adobe Acrobat.

Modern browser required

Use a recent version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. Internet Explorer isn't supported.

MOD_09 // CONVERT

PDF to Word

Turn a PDF into a Word document (.docx).

Editable text gives you text you can change, with headings and paragraphs, but a simpler layout. Exact look keeps every page exactly as it looks, but the text can't be edited.

Limitations for this tool
  • Editable mode keeps the text, headings and paragraphs, but not columns, tables, pictures or exact positions.
  • Scanned PDFs have no real text inside. For those, MyPDF switches to Exact look automatically (it can't read text from images).
  • Password-protected PDFs aren't supported.
  • Fonts in the Word file may differ from the PDF.

Word to PDF

Turn a Word document (.docx) into a PDF.

Download PDF works on every device. Print / Save as PDF uses your browser's print window and keeps the text selectable. Choose "Save as PDF" as the printer.

Preview
Limitations for this tool
  • Only .docx files are supported. For old .doc files, open them in Word or Google Docs and save as .docx first.
  • Fonts that aren't on your device are replaced with similar ones, so line breaks can shift.
  • Download PDF saves each page as a sharp picture, so text in it can't be selected or searched. Use Print / Save as PDF for selectable text.
  • Comments, tracked changes, text boxes and some charts or shapes may be left out or look different.

Why choose MyPDF?

MyPDF is a free online PDF toolkit that runs entirely in your web browser. Unlike many other PDF sites, MyPDF never uploads your documents to a server. Converting, merging, splitting and signing all happen on your own computer or phone, so your contracts, invoices and personal files stay private.

How to use each tool

JPG to PDF

  1. Open JPG to PDF and drop your photos or scans in (JPG, PNG, WebP and more).
  2. Drag the cards to put the pages in order.
  3. Choose the page size (same as image, A4 or US Letter), orientation and margin.
  4. Click Convert to PDF and download your file.

PDF to JPG

  1. Open PDF to JPG and drop in a PDF.
  2. Pick JPG or PNG.
  3. Choose the quality: Normal, High or Very high. Use High on phones for long documents.
  4. Download each image, or everything at once as a ZIP file.

Merge PDF

  1. Open Merge and add two or more PDFs.
  2. Drag the files into the order you want.
  3. Click Merge PDF to join them into one document.
  4. Download the combined PDF.

Split PDF

  1. Open Split and drop in a PDF.
  2. Choose how: every page as its own PDF, one PDF per range, or chosen pages extracted into one PDF.
  3. Type the pages or ranges, such as 1-3, 5, 8-10, and click Split PDF.
  4. Download the new files one by one or as a ZIP.

PDF to Word

  1. Open PDF to Word and drop in a PDF.
  2. Choose Editable text to change the words, or Exact look to keep every page as it is.
  3. Click Convert to Word and open the .docx in Word or Google Docs.

Word, Excel or PowerPoint to PDF

  1. Open the matching tool and drop in your .docx, .xlsx or .pptx file.
  2. Check the preview. For Excel, pick the sheets, orientation and paper size.
  3. Click Download PDF, or Print / Save as PDF for selectable text (Word and Excel).

Organize pages

  1. Open Organize pages and drop in a PDF.
  2. Rotate pages with ↺ ↻, delete with ✕, and drag cards (or use ◀ ▶) to reorder.
  3. Click Save PDF.

Compress PDF

  1. Open Compress and drop in a PDF.
  2. Pick Strong, Balanced or Light.
  3. See how much smaller it got, then download.

Page numbers & watermark

  1. Open the tool and drop in a PDF.
  2. Choose where the numbers go and their format, and type a watermark if you want one.
  3. Check the preview, then click Apply & download.

Sign PDF

  1. Open Sign and drop in the PDF you need to sign.
  2. Draw, type or upload your signature.
  3. Tap the page where it should go, then resize and move it.
  4. Download the signed PDF.

Good to know

Everyday uses. People use MyPDF to turn phone photos of receipts and IDs into one PDF for an application, to pull a single page out of a long contract, to combine scanned forms before emailing them, to sign a lease or an HR form without printing it, to turn a Word résumé into a PDF, to shrink a scan so it fits an email limit, and to stamp page numbers on a report.

Why "no upload" matters. Most online PDF sites send your file to their server, process it there and send it back. That means a copy of your document sits on someone else's computer, at least for a while. MyPDF does the same work with JavaScript inside your browser tab, so there is no copy anywhere else. You can even switch off your internet after the page loads and the tools keep working.

Tips for best results. For clear PDFs from photos, shoot in good light and crop the edges first. When splitting, check the page count shown on screen before typing ranges. For very large files, use a laptop or desktop, which has more memory than a phone.

Frequently asked questions

Is MyPDF really free?

Yes. Every tool is free to use with no sign-up and no watermarks.

Are my files safe?

Your files never leave your device. All processing happens locally in your browser.

Is the signature legally binding?

MyPDF adds a visual signature image to your PDF. This is enough for many everyday documents. Some legal documents require a certified digital signature, so check the requirements for your document.

Can I convert Word, Excel and PowerPoint files?

Yes. MyPDF turns .docx, .xlsx, .xls, .csv and .pptx files into PDF, and turns PDFs into Word (.docx) or plain text. Everything happens in your browser, so even these files are never uploaded. Complex layouts may come out simpler than the original, so check the preview.

How much smaller does Compress make my PDF?

Scans and photo-heavy PDFs often shrink by half or more. PDFs that are mostly text may not get smaller, and MyPDF tells you when that happens so you can keep your original.

Do I need to install anything?

No. MyPDF works in any modern browser on Windows, Mac, Android, iPhone and Linux. There is nothing to download or install.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once the page has fully loaded, every tool works without an internet connection, because nothing is sent to a server.

Will my PDF have a watermark?

No. MyPDF never adds watermarks, logos or extra pages to your files.

Is there a file size limit?

There is no fixed limit, but very large files depend on your device's memory. See Known limitations.
